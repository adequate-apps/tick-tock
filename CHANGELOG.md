# Changelog

## [2.0.0]

- Tune wind instruments with written or concert note names, adjustable A4, pitch trace and an
  ears-first mode. Capture per-note intonation statistics while listening.
- Play a drone and reference tones. Set metronome tempo, metre, accents, subdivisions and gap bars.
  Calibrate the visual offset for a Bluetooth output.
- Compare session and history distributions in Table. Save practice labels, inspect individual notes
  and share CSV statistics through Android's share sheet.
- Choose Gridline, Daylight, Dark or Pit themes, larger text and settled-note announcements.
- Import and export personal settings, inspect audio diagnostics and read bundled licence notices.
- Process audio and keep history on the device. Microphone access is optional for drone and metronome.

Android 9 or later, on arm64-v8a or x86_64. Playback and listening stop when the app moves to the
background. The APK and Play delivery use the same application signing identity when enrolled as
described in the release runbook.
