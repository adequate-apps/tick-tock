# Privacy policy

AdequateApps develops Adequate Metronome. Updated 2026-10-15.

Adequate Metronome processes microphone audio on your phone to measure pitch and show tuning information.
Microphone access is optional. The drone and metronome work with microphone access denied.

Audio samples stay in working memory while the app listens. The app stores pitch readings, session
names, equipment labels, notes and settings in its private storage. Raw pitch readings from unsaved
sessions age out after seven days. Readings in saved sessions and per-note statistics remain until
you delete the session or clear the relevant history. The app stops listening when it moves to the
background.

The app has no network permission, advertising, analytics, account system or cloud backup. The
developer receives no microphone audio, practice history or settings through the app.

You control sharing. Share CSV sends the selected statistics to the application you choose through
Android's share sheet. Export settings writes a configuration document to the destination you choose.
Those documents can contain your session labels, equipment or horn nicknames. The receiving application
or storage provider handles a shared document under its own policy.

A crash report stays on the phone. You may choose to share it. Settings provides a way to
clear all practice history. Uninstalling the app removes its private history and settings; documents
you previously exported remain at the destinations you chose.

The Privacy policy button opens this page in your browser. The browser and its website connection
operate under their own policies.

For a privacy inquiry, open an issue in the
[Adequate Metronome project](https://github.com/adequate-apps/tick-tock/issues).
Issues are public. Keep microphone recordings, exported practice data and other personal information
out of the issue; describe the question in general terms. GitHub handles account and website data
under its own privacy policy.
